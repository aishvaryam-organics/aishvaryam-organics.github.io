Our cows

Two breeds that belong to Gujarat.

Gir and Kankrej cattle have lived on this land for centuries. Both are indigenous Indian breeds, and both give A2 milk. Our herd is purebred, and we never breed related animals with each other.

A2

What A2 milk means

Cow's milk contains a protein called beta-casein. It comes in two main types, A1 and A2. Indigenous Indian breeds such as Gir and Kankrej carry the A2 type, which is why their milk is called A2 milk. Ours comes only from these two breeds.

How we milk

Calf first. Then us.

Pehle bachhda, phir hum.

  1. The calf drinks.Every milking begins with the calf. It drinks until it is full.
  2. We milk by hand.No machine touches our cows. Each one is milked by hand.
  3. You get what is left.Only the milk that remains is taken for people. We follow this strictly, every day.

The farm

Raised near Gandhinagar, Gujarat's green capital.

Our farm is close to Gandhinagar, a city known for its trees. The herd is free-range here. The cows walk, graze and rest in the open.

The same care goes into the land. We grow the herd's green fodder ourselves, organically, and we do not plant hybrid grass.

Questions

Ask us anything.

Can I buy your milk or ghee today?

Not yet. We are introducing the herd first. Leave your details above and you will hear from us before anyone else when A2 milk and milk products are ready.

Why only Gir and Kankrej?

Both are indigenous breeds from Gujarat, so they suit this land and climate. Both give A2 milk. We keep the herd purebred, and we never breed related animals with each other.

Why do you care so much about feed?

You are what you eat, and that applies to cows too. Our green fodder is organic and grown in-house. Our whole cotton seed is organic, rain-fed, and bought directly from farmers. We do not feed hybrid grass or Napier.

Do the calves really drink first?

Yes, at every milking. The calf drinks its fill, and only the milk that is left is used for people. We follow this strictly.

Do you use milking machines, hormone injections or antibiotics?

No. Our cows are milked by hand. They are not given hormone injections, and they are not given antibiotics.
